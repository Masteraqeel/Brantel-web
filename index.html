Brantel Connect

It starts with a phone.

In 2004 Brantel became Lenovo's agent for Sri Lanka. In 2008 it launched its own brand, E-TEL, which grew into the country's second best selling smartphone. Today Brantel Connect sells phones and tablets through dealers and service centres across the island.

Brands include E-TEL, Blackview and Oukitel

Brantel Energy

It runs up to the roof.

Brantel Energy supplies and installs solar for factories, warehouses and homes, from rooftop systems to ground mounted arrays of 500 kW. It holds national distribution for GoodWe, SMA, Deye, SolarEdge and JA Solar, and trains installers on roadshows around the island.

Recent sites: Winsland Reality 500 kW, Coldcargo 250 kW, Friend Polypack 200 kW

Networks and IT

It runs through the building.

Since 2014 Brantel has supplied servers, mini PCs and thin clients to companies, and Corning optical cabling and Cisco networks to buildings across Sri Lanka, among them Lotus Tower, Havelock City and Mattala airport.

Also hospitals, universities, resorts and government offices

NorthWest Marine Lanka

It runs out to sea.

NorthWest Marine has built fibreglass boats since 2006, first at Dankotuwa and since 2023 at its own yard beside Dikkowita fishery harbour. Hulls run from 6 metre day boats to 20 metre multi day longliners, for Sri Lankan owners and for export.

Also ice plants, fish processing plants and vessel maintenance

Brantel Mineral Exploration and Mining Services

It runs down into the ground.

The mining company maps, surveys, drills and assays ground for heavy mineral sands, chromite, manganese, copper and iron ore. It works on the group's own projects and for other licence holders, from offices in Sri Lanka and Oman.

Own drill rigs, XRF and AAS assay, feasibility studies and EIAs

Brantel Group

Five businesses, one group.

Each company holds its own licences, staff and customers. What they share is a habit of supplying the equipment, installing it and looking after it afterwards.
