Energy and technology

Brantel Lanka

Supplying the corporate sector since 2004

The company entered the market as appointed agent for Lenovo, launched its own E-TEL device brand in 2008, and opened an enterprise division in 2014 for servers, thin clients, mini PCs and custom software.

It is national distributor for Corning, Cisco, GoodWe, SMA, Deye, SolarEdge and JA Solar among others, with branches, dealers and service centres across the island and technical teams who commission what they sell.

  • Rooftop and ground mounted solar to 500 kW
  • Corning optical and Cisco structured cabling
  • Smartboards, servers, mini PCs and thin clients
  • Installer training roadshows nationwide

Marine manufacturing

NorthWest Marine Lanka

Built at Dikkowita, sold beyond it

The company started in 2006 at the Industrial Development Board estate in Dankotuwa with a small team of craftsmen and the aim of building longline fibreglass hulls good enough to export. In 2023 it moved into a purpose built yard opposite Dikkowita International Fisheries Harbour.

Alongside the boats it provides marine engineering to the Sri Lankan fishing community: maintenance contracts, vessel operation contracts, longline advisory work, ice and fish processing plant installation, and supply of gear, engines, generators and spares.

  • Fishing, workboat, passenger and leisure hulls
  • Multi day longline vessels for offshore work
  • Ice plants and fish processing plants installed
  • Project planning for the fisheries sector

Working for the fleet that works the coast

Most of what leaves the yard goes to Sri Lankan owners running multi day trips out of harbours like this one. The engineering side of the business keeps those vessels at sea, with maintenance and operation contracts, gear and spares, and advisory work on longline methods.

The same capability builds the shore infrastructure behind the fleet, from ice plants and fish processing plants to project planning for the sector.

Mineral resources

Exploration and mining

Sri Lanka and Oman, working three regions

Brantel Mineral Exploration and Mining Services runs exploration programmes on the group's own projects and under contract to other licence holders, targeting heavy mineral sands, chromite, manganese, copper and iron ore.

Geological, engineering and metallurgical staff have come from working mines around the world. Projects are mostly open pit heavy mineral sand and industrial mineral deposits, with project work reaching across Africa, Australasia and the Middle East.

  • Company owned drill rigs, logged and sampled in house
  • Full geophysical suite from electrical to gravity
  • XRF and AAS analysis, field and laboratory
  • Technical reporting and environmental assessment

What the group looks for

Heavy mineral sands, chromite, manganese, copper and iron ore. Targets are assessed on grade, tonnage and the cost of moving the product to a port, rather than on headline resource figures alone.

Every programme runs to the same sequence: map the ground, survey it, drill it, assay the samples, then decide whether the deposit carries a mine.

Capability

Ground to feasibility, in house.

A target can be mapped, drilled, assayed and modelled without handing the programme to anyone else.

Field mapping

Geological and structural mapping to identify new deposits and set drill targets.

Geophysical investigation

Electrical, electromagnetic, magnetic, ground penetrating radar and gravity surveys across prospective zones.

Drilling

Programmes run on owned or contracted rigs, with core and chips logged, sampled and modelled through to a block model.

Sample analysis

XRF for oxides in field and laboratory, AAS for elements, grain counting for heavy mineral sand assemblages.

Studies and permitting

Economic assessment, pre feasibility and feasibility studies, reserve estimation and environmental impact assessment.

Mine planning

Strategic mine planning and engineering for open pit deposits, active mining and operational audit.
