Brantel Connect

It starts with a phone.

Brantel began in 2004 as Lenovo's agent for Sri Lanka and launched its own brand, E-TEL, in 2008. E-TEL grew into the country's second best selling smartphone. Brantel Connect sells phones and tablets through dealers and service centres across the island.

2004
Lenovo agency for Sri Lanka
2008
E-TEL brand launched
3
Handset brands: E-TEL, Blackview, Oukitel

Brantel Energy

It runs along the array.

Brantel Energy supplies and installs solar for factories, warehouses and homes, and distributes GoodWe, SMA, Deye, SolarEdge and JA Solar across the island.

Networks and IT

It runs into the rack.

Since 2014 Brantel has supplied servers, mini PCs and thin clients to companies, and Corning optical cabling and Cisco networks to buildings across Sri Lanka, among them Lotus Tower, Havelock City and Mattala airport.

2014
Enterprise division opened
20+

NorthWest Marine Lanka

It runs out to sea.

NorthWest Marine has built fibreglass boats since 2006, first at Dankotuwa and since 2023 at its own yard beside Dikkowita fishery harbour, for Sri Lankan owners and for export.

2006
Founded at Dankotuwa
6 to 20 m
Hull range, day boats to longliners
2023
Dikkowita yard opened

Brantel Mineral Exploration and Mining Services

It runs down into the ground.

The mining company maps, surveys, drills and assays ground for heavy mineral sands, chromite, manganese, copper and iron ore, on the group's own projects and for other licence holders.

Five businesses, one group.

Each company holds its own licences, staff and customers. What they share is a habit of supplying the equipment, installing it and looking after it afterwards.
